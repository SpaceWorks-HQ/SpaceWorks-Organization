# Landing page (`/`)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: organisation office-bearers and self-hosters, equally. Main action: apply at `/start`; secondary: self-host from GitHub. Proof: sample data only, built around Shaan Shoukath and SpaceWorks, labelled as a sample; no customer or usage claims. Sister reference: space-works.netlify.app (cream sections, deep-ink and teal full-bleed bands, two-line full-stop headlines, orange primary action with arrow, "sample data" product frames, never-edited record timelines, terminal install block).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one member's year. A sample member card for Shaan Shoukath of SpaceWorks travels April to March, and every product capability appears where it happens in that year. It refuses the icon-card feature grid.

OWN-WORLD: Space Works brand only. Warm Cream ground with the faint isometric box pattern, a Deep Ink band and a Maker Teal band at full bleed, Citron for "valid" and checks, Sky Blue and Warm Yellow as small authored objects (reminder email, receipt highlight), Workshop Orange only on the primary action. Gilroy ExtraBold headlines in two short full-stop lines, Gilroy Light body, one Unbounded phrase. Hairline borders, pill controls, tabular numerals for money and receipt numbers.

STORY: Visitors see a real-looking card deal in and flip to its QR, understand the platform runs the whole membership year (join, approve, pay, receipt, card, remind, renew), believe it because the receipt maths and gapless numbers are shown, see it scales to units and reports, learn they can run it themselves, and apply.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, about half width: headline "Your members. In good standing." in two Gilroy ExtraBold lines; subline; orange "Apply for your organisation" and a text link "Run it yourself"; a quiet line "Free and open source. Self-hosted. In active development." (product truth: nothing is released yet, so it says so). Right: a dealt deck of three objects (receipt slip at the back showing its total, the teal member card in front, a renewal reminder below showing its dateline); under the deck, an April-to-March ruler with April lit and a "Turn over" control that flips the card to its QR side. The page's one Unbounded phrase is "One card." in the year section: Unbounded's width at display size breaks the two-line hero on phones.

FORM: Structure "One member's year", fifth on my ranked list of seven, dealt to lead by seed key aa9a27a7; locked by the owner.

Signature interaction: the deck deals in once on load (staggered, exponential ease-out), the card flips in 3D to its verification side on demand, and the year ruler fills as the year section scrolls, with marks at the months where the timeline stops fall. Supporting motion, each tied to its object: the receipt prints top first once, and the terminal caret blinks. Everything rests in its final state under reduced motion and without scroll-driven animation support.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
